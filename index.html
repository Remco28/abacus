A QUICK START

Meet your soroban.

Each rod holds one digit. Move beads toward the middle bar to count them: the upper bead is worth 5, and each lower bead is worth 1. Counted beads glow green.

Slide a bead to push its neighbors, or tap to bring it to the bar. Use several fingers to move different beads together.

The small dots on the bar mark every third rod. Tap a place label, or use Ones column, to change decimal placement. The number on the board changes; the beads stay put.

Enable shake, allow motion access if asked, then shake three times within two seconds to clear. Undo brings the board back. Sound starts after your first interaction.

Keyboard: Tab to a bead and press Enter or Space to move it. Your board and preferences are saved on this device.

To keep it close: use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home Screen option. After the first online visit, it works offline.
