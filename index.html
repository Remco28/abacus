Help

Motion not working?

Android in Chrome or Brave. Tap the icon left of the address bar → Permissions → set Motion sensors to Allow, then reload this page. If it is not listed there, open the browser menu (the three dots at the top right) → Settings → Site settings → Motion sensors and allow it. Brave blocks motion sensors by default, so the per-site Allow is what turns shake on. The page cannot override either setting.

Android in another browser. Allow motion sensors when the browser asks, then return here and try Enable again.

iPhone. Allow Motion & Orientation Access when Safari asks. If it never asks, open Settings → Safari and turn on Motion & Orientation Access — under Privacy & Security or Advanced, depending on the iOS version. If readings still do not arrive, open this page in Safari.

Computer. Most laptops and desktops have no motion sensor, so shake needs a phone or tablet.

If this page opened inside another app — Facebook, Instagram, Gmail — use that app’s menu to open the page in your browser instead: those viewers cannot read the sensors. Clear always works without motion.

How it works

Move beads toward the middle bar to count them. The upper bead is worth 5; each lower bead is worth 1. Counted beads glow green.

Slide to push neighboring beads, or tap to move a group. Several fingers can move beads together.

Tap the lock beside Clear to unlock the gold caret, drag it along the bar to move the decimal, then release to lock it again. The dots mark every third rod.

Keyboard: Tab to a bead and press Enter or Space. Unlock the decimal control before using its arrow keys. Your board and sound preference are saved on this device.

Use Install app or Add to Home Screen to keep it close. After the first online visit, it works offline.

Install on your phone

Give the board more room: install Soroban and open it from your home-screen icon. It opens in its own app window without the browser’s address bar or tabs. Your phone’s status bar or gesture area may still be visible.

Android: open your browser’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Choose Install if offered, then launch Soroban from its icon.

iPhone: open this site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add. Launch Soroban from the new icon.

After an online visit, the installed app also works offline.
